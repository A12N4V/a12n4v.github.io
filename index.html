A mask is a sign.

IV  ·  Historia

From sign to machine

The lineage this work comes from: signs, then feedback, then computing with the stuff of life, then machines that learn. After 2023 the machines start to dream.

  1. Sema

    the science of signs

  2. c. 1867

    Peirce's signs

    Charles Sanders Peirce begins his theory of signs. A sign can resemble its object (icon), be caused by it (index) or stand for it by convention (symbol).

  3. 1916

    Signifier, signified

    Saussure's Course in General Linguistics, published after his death: the word arbor and the idea of a tree are two sides of one sign.

  4. Cybernetica

    steering by feedback

  5. 1943

    The logical neuron

    McCulloch and Pitts model the neuron as a threshold unit. Nervous systems and logic become one subject.

  6. 1948

    Cybernetics

    Norbert Wiener names the field: control and communication in the animal and the machine.

  7. Bio-computatio

    computing with the stuff of life

  8. 1952

    The action potential, in equations

    Hodgkin and Huxley write the nerve impulse of the squid giant axon as four coupled differential equations.

  9. 1952

    Morphogenesis

    Alan Turing shows how two reacting, diffusing chemicals can break symmetry into spots and stripes.

  10. 1972

    Brain of the Firm

    Stafford Beer's Viable System Model: any system that survives has the same five-part structure of control.

  11. 1994

    Computing with DNA

    Leonard Adleman solves a seven-node Hamiltonian path problem in a test tube, with strands of DNA as the processor.

  12. 2003

    The genome, read

    The Human Genome Project is declared complete.

  13. Machina

    machines that learn

  14. 2012

    AlexNet

    A deep convolutional network wins ImageNet by a wide margin, and deep learning takes over vision.

  15. 2015

    DeepDream

    Run a vision network backwards and it starts to see dogs and eyes in everything. The first machine hallucinations are pictures.

  16. 2017

    Attention

    "Attention Is All You Need" introduces the transformer.

  17. 2020

    Protein folding

    AlphaFold 2 predicts protein structures at close to experimental accuracy at CASP14.

  18. 2022

    ChatGPT

    Language models reach everyone.

  19. 2023

    Hallucinate

    Cambridge Dictionary's word of the year: when an artificial intelligence hallucinates, it produces false information. The machines begin to dream out loud.

  20. 2024

    The prize for structure

    The Nobel Prize in Chemistry goes to David Baker, Demis Hassabis and John Jumper for protein design and structure prediction. In physics, to John Hopfield and Geoffrey Hinton for the foundations of neural networks.

  21. 2025

    Signal or sign?

    Models write, see, speak and act. What they produce is a sign of the world, and sometimes a sign of nothing at all.

  22. MMXXVI

    Homonin

    Grounding the model in the body. Every sign a twin produces should be checked against the signal the body gives back.
